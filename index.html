About

A strong foundation in basics paves the way for success, especially in mastering technical subjects. When students grasp concepts with clarity, it makes all the difference.

I believe every individual has unique talents, and it's the teacher's role to nurture and bring out the best in them. My focus is always on reinforcing fundamentals while encouraging students to chart their own paths — learning is an art, distinct from mere studying.

"Enjoy the journey of learning."

Since 2016 I've taught microprocessor architecture, digital electronics, and embedded systems as Assistant Professor at Anna University, alongside 7+ years mentoring students online in assembly language and microcontroller programming.
